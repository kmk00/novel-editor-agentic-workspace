The rain had stopped by the time Rei reached the station.

Momo: [<<Calling out across the platform, light and teasing>> You're late again.]

Rei: [<<Answering with a flat and tired voice>> I know. The train was stuck between two tunnels.]

He did not turn around. He already knew that voice.

Momo: [<<Replying with a dry and unimpressed tone>> Then stop blaming the train.]

A puddle shivered under the streetlight.

Momo: [<<Inviting him warmly, as if the argument is already over>> Come on. I'll buy you a coffee.]

CHAPTER ONE. THE LATE TRAIN.
